Our mini farm

Raised from babies and loved every day.

We are a small family-owned mini farm located near Rome, Georgia. Our flock includes 17 chickens, 7 ducks, 2 geese, and 2 turkeys. We started with all of them as babies in January and February and have loved raising them every step of the way.

We truly love our birds, and the money from every egg purchase goes right back into caring for them through food, bedding, treats, and improvements to their home.

Thank you for giving us a chance, supporting our small farm, and helping us continue doing what we love.
